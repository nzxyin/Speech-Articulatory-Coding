Instructions

Listener , short trials, about 20-25 minutes. Use headphones in a quiet room and keep the volume fixed.

Each trial has a Reference (a real recording of a person) and two synthetic recordings, A and B, that say the same sentence, which is a different sentence from the reference. Ignore what is said and the recording quality of the reference. Judge who seems to be speaking.

Play every clip to the end at least once. You may replay clips. For each of A and B give two ratings, then choose the one that sounds more like the same person as the Reference.

Similarity: 1 = clearly a different person, 5 = clearly the same person. Naturalness: 1 = very unnatural or distorted, 5 = completely natural.
