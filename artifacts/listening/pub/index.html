Whose voice is it?

Pilot listening test

You will hear a real person, then two computer-generated recordings. Your job is to judge which recording sounds more like that same person. About 34 short trials, 20 to 25 minutes. Please use headphones in a quiet room and keep the volume fixed.

Start or continue

Pick the listener number the study host gave you. If you stop part-way, open this page again and you continue where you left off.

How each trial works

The Reference is a real recording of a person. A and B are synthetic and say a different sentence. Ignore what is said and the recording quality of the Reference; judge who seems to be speaking.

Play every clip to the end at least once (replaying is fine). Rate A and B, then choose the one that sounds more like the same person as the Reference.

Similarity: 1 = clearly a different person, 5 = clearly the same person. Naturalness: 1 = very unnatural or distorted, 5 = completely natural.
